About the Gallery

Founded 2018 · Berlin · Online

Monolith is an independent online gallery devoted exclusively to monochrome contemporary art. We believe that the absence of color is not a limitation — it is a discipline, a focus, an invitation to see structure and silence.

Our roster spans twelve countries and three generations of artists, all working at the intersection of geometry, minimalism, and conceptual practice. Every work in our collection is selected by our curatorial board through a closed nomination process.

We exhibit only online. The screen, like the gallery wall, is a frame — and one accessible to anyone, anywhere.
